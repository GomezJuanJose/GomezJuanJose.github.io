Puzzle game that shows AI behavior and a quadrupedal movement system

Description

A 3d platform and puzzle game with stylized aesthetics where you guide a wolf guardian through a forgotten and beautiful world to help a little puppy. Enter this small world to face its challenges and dangers to unravel its mysteries.

Features

  • Quadrupedal movement system.
  • A single world without loading screens.
  • AI enemies using behavior trees.
  • UI system.
  • Compatible with controller.
  • Physics puzzles.
  • Translated to english and spanish.
